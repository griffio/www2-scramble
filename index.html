SCRAMBLE!

The Luftwaffe is forming up over Cap Gris-Nez. Take your flight off from RAF Hawkinge, smash their raids, then carry the fight across the Channel — destroy the hangars and crater the runway at Audembert, and sweep their squadrons from the sky.

Historically inspired aircraft and tactics, with compressed geography and arcade flight, damage and weapon envelopes. Full sortie starts airborne; quick start skips to the action. C changes camera; F checks six. Stay with the aircraft you are protecting.

  • Lead three AI wingmen in your twin-engine Beaufighter
  • Fly the Channel crossing and Dutch approach using waypoint guidance
  • Release 2 × 500 lb bombs yourself, then escape the eight-second delay fuzes
  • Evade AA tracers and heavy flak; strafe gun positions to silence them

Inspired by the Philips works raid at Eindhoven. An imagined Beaufighter sortie with compressed geography and an arcade bomb load.
